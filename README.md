# 🛒 Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, product categories, order status, regional sales, and monthly trends.

The dashboard helps transform sales data into meaningful insights to support better business decisions.

## 🖼️ Dashboard Preview

![Ecommerce Sales Dashboard](images/ecommerce-sales-dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze yearly sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across different products and states.
- Monitor order status, including delivered, cancelled, returned, and shipped orders.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare salesperson performance.
- Present key business metrics in an interactive dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Category Analysis:** Highlights the top five product categories.
- **Yearly Sales:** Compares sales performance for 2024 and 2025.
- **Product Analysis:** Compares sales across laptops, LED TVs, smartphones, tablets, and water purifiers.
- **State-wise Sales:** Displays sales performance across selected Indian states.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Shows the distribution of cancelled, delivered, returned, and shipped orders.
- **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Tracks sales trends from January to December.
- **Salesperson Analysis:** Compares the sales contribution of different salespeople.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filters data by order date, month, and product category.
